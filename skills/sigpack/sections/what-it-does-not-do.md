## What this skill does not do

Say this once, early, whenever the request reaches for it. Not as an apology —
as the scope.

- **It does not look at a returned page.** No verdict on whether a block is signed, partial, blank, unclear or from the wrong version. Nothing in the reply may say a page is signed.
- **It does not match returns to pages, or place them.** No executed set, no page-for-page replacement, no counterpart assembly, no spare originals filed.
- **It does not merge documents into one file**, so there is no pack PDF and no executed PDF.
- **It does not balance anything.** There is no count of signed against required, because there is nothing to count it from. The standing line says what was drafted, what was sent and what the lawyer has told you came back.
- **It does not date pages**, or add a date to anything already signed.

The reason is the same in every case: the original skill looked at every returned
page as an image before it compiled anything, and that looking is what made the
executed set honest. Doing the assembly without it would be the less safe tool.
So this skill does the half it can do properly, and the lawyer, or a colleague
with the pages in front of them, does the other half.

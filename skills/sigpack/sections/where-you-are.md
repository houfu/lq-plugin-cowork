## Where the closing is when you arrive

No arguments, no modes and no grammar to learn. Route on what the lawyer puts in
front of you and confirm the door in one line before starting.

| What they have | What you do |
|---|---|
| Execution versions and no signature pages | Read the signing matrix out of them, confirm it, draft the pages |
| Signature pages already drafted | Skip the drafting; build the per-party sets, the instruction table and the cover note from the pages they supply |
| Pages out and some back | Update the ledger they attach, and draft the chasers for what is outstanding |
| Signed pages and a request to assemble the executed documents | Say plainly this skill does not do that, and why, in one line — then offer the parts it does do |

Plain words in the request count as instructions: "packs per counterparty, two
copies" settles grouping and copies without anyone being asked. An explicit
instruction wins over a `[sigpack]` playbook default, which wins over asking.
Only ask for what neither settles.

## There is no automation here

Manual add, ask, browse and check are the complete product. Nothing runs in the
background: notes are brought in when the lawyer asks for them, and saved when
the lawyer asks for that. Do not ask about capture, matter labels or automatic
retrieval during setup, and do not offer to turn anything on.

If the user asks whether the wiki can save or retrieve on its own, answer in one
line and move on: "It saves when you ask it to, and it looks things up when you
ask it to. There is nothing to switch on." You can add that they can ask for the
reusable lessons from the conversation in front of them with one request.

For a first-use walkthrough or starter prompts, use
[Getting started](references/getting-started.md).

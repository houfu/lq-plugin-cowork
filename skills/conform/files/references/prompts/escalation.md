# Writing the escalation reason

A checklist for step 4. This step never re-decides a mapping. It turns a mapping that already escalates into one plain sentence a lawyer can act on.

Whether a mapping escalates is fixed by the rule in the skill body and is not a judgment made here:

- `false_friend`, `one_to_many`, `many_to_one`, `no_mapping`, `needs_review`, `insufficient_evidence`, `narrower_scope`, `broader_scope` and `leakage_flag` always escalate.
- `equivalent` escalates only where its supporting evidence is incomplete.

## How to write the sentence

Name what the lawyer has to decide, in the language of the deal. Prefer "your agreement splits this source concept into two defined terms — confirm both are needed here" over "one-to-many mapping requires escalation". Prefer "the precedent's definition carries a carve-out yours does not, so substituting the term would widen the obligation" over "narrower scope".

One sentence. No internal decision labels, no mapping identifiers, no talk of stages or queues.

Never write a reason that could be read as clearance to proceed without review. Every escalated mapping stays a proposal until the lawyer accepts, edits or rejects it, and the sentence should read that way.

Where a decision was unresolved rather than mapped, the sentence says what was missing — the definition you could not find, the schedule that was not supplied — so the lawyer knows what would settle it.

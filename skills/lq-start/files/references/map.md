# The map — every skill by the situation that calls for it

Read before naming any skill. Every entry says what the skill is for, what it
is not for, and the neighbour to use instead. That is the routing.

There are two LegalQuants bundles for Copilot Cowork:

- **LegalQuants litigation skills** — source-grounded workflows for litigators.
- **LegalQuants transactional skills** — contract and deal workflows.

The first group below is in both bundles; the other two belong to one bundle
each, as their headings say. Every entry carries its bundle, so name any of them
freely and say which bundle it belongs to.

## In both bundles

- **You do not know which skill fits** → lq-start. This map, and one pick with
  the sentence to type. It routes; it never does the work, and it is not itself
  a destination.
- **Completed work needs explaining to a client, a board or a colleague** →
  legaldesign. One editable, evidence-grounded page or slide
  brief, built from work already done, that opens in the preview pane. It lays
  out content that already exists; it does not write the substance. Not a
  formatted report or deck: for those, the built-in Word and PowerPoint
  skills.
- **Time entries need narratives** → timenarratives. Drafts candidate
  narratives for the named lawyer and matter from this conversation and the
  documents you supply, as an unvalidated preview. Deal or dispute, same skill.
  No hours, no rates, no billability calls, and not for case chronologies or
  fact narratives. Private practice only; in-house lawyers skip this.
- **You want to keep what you learned** → wiki. Your personal legal wiki in a
  folder you own: linked, source-grounded notes that record reusable law and
  method, never matter facts. The layer underneath everything. Not a place for
  client or party facts, matter chronologies or work product.
- **The deal needs a working closing checklist, or the one you have needs
  updating** → closing-checklist. Drafts an editable checklist from the share
  purchase agreement or other anchor agreement, or proposes and applies changes
  to your existing one after revised agreements or new instructions, keeping
  house formatting, references and timing. It tracks deliverables and conditions
  to closing: it does not review a draft against negotiation positions, chase
  status, build signature packs or audit the closing folder.
- **You want to know where you stand with AI** → lq-mirror. Twelve short
  questions and an honest reading — your archetype, said in a way that respects
  you. Recognition, never a verdict, never a score, and never the legal work
  itself.

## Litigation bundle

- **A new dispute needs a controlled start** → new-matter. Captures the matter,
  forum, posture, authority, conflicts status, urgent dates and preservation
  needs before anything is written or moved. Organising the documents that
  follow is organize-case-docs.
- **The case file needs a working map** → organize-case-docs. Builds the source
  inventory, chronology, proof chart, issue and evidence maps and matter
  briefing without moving originals. Creating the matter record first is
  new-matter.
- **In U.S. federal civil practice, you are planning preservation, serving
  requests, answering them, or handling a subpoena** → document-discovery.
  Builds a proportionate preservation package, or drafts targeted requests,
  itemised responses and objections, meet-and-confer positions, privilege
  routing and subpoena triage. Not for reviewing a production you have
  received.
- **A deposition needs preparing or reconciling** → depositions. Turns the
  proof plan into witness, topic, exhibit, admission and impeachment work,
  including Rule 30(b)(6), nonparty and transcript follow-through.
- **A brief, regulator paper or client memorandum needs drafting** → writing.
  Chooses advocacy or neutral analysis, then writes from the record with the
  right candour, authority and review controls. Checking the citations
  afterwards is cite-check.
- **A position or the other side's case needs to survive contact** →
  pressuretest. Maps the argument, confirms the map with you, attacks it every
  way the material allows, and reports where it breaks — logic, dates, figures
  and cross-document consistency only. Only when you expressly ask for it to be
  pressure-tested. Citations stay with cite-check; legal research does not
  belong here.
- **A filing cites authorities you have in hand** → cite-check. Verifies every
  citation and quotation against the authorities you supply, and catches
  fabricated case law before filing. It never guesses at a source it was not
  given, and it does not determine whether an authority is still good law.
- **A litigation letter or settlement exchange needs drafting** →
  correspondence. Handles discovery, substantive and settlement correspondence
  with source, formation and no-send review gates. Sending is yours; the
  built-in Email skill does that.
- **The client, co-counsel or portfolio owner needs the status** →
  client-update. Separates verified developments, analysis, recommendations and
  decisions in matter, event, outside-counsel and portfolio formats.

## Transactional bundle

- **You need to turn approved precedents into reusable positions** →
  playbook-builder. Builds a source-linked contract playbook in Markdown with
  preferred positions, fallbacks, red lines and lawyer-curated Matter Lenses,
  or imports the playbook table your team already keeps. It does not approve
  inferred policy for you, and it does not review live paper.
- **A live draft needs checking against that approved playbook** →
  playbook-review. Starts from Standard Baseline, applies only the Matter
  Lenses you actively choose, and returns a clause-anchored issues list with
  suggested inline markup and coverage receipts. It accepts substantially
  equivalent drafting. It does not produce a tracked-changes redline.

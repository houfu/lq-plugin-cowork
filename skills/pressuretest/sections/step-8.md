## Step 8 — Check the result before delivering

Check the record and the rendered output against each other before anything
reaches the lawyer. Re-derive the verdict from the adjudication table rather
than restating one settled earlier. Confirm that the coverage partition
accounts for every selected document, including unreadable, excluded and
parked items; that every Pressure Point names its position owner and the limb
it hits; that every flip or defect statement is present and no two Pressure
Points carry the same flip statement; and that an unanswered interactive
checkpoint has not been treated as authority for findings or for a completed
verdict. The source inventory and every visible pinpoint are required, and
their presence is not their accuracy. Compare the full rendered text against
the record: a changed or omitted finding or quote is a fault, not a formatting
choice. The substantive method is identical on the report and the chat path.

Verify each quotation by locating it in the source text. Text, Markdown and
Word sources allow that directly; other formats, including PDF, may not, and a
quote that cannot be located is reported individually as unverified. A located
quote means normalised text occurrence only — matching after unicode
normalisation, typographic quote and dash substitution, and collapsed
whitespace. It does not verify authorship, pinpoint accuracy, context,
entailment, legal correctness or currentness, and the report keeps "pinpoints
verified" and "entailment verified" explicitly false.

Report the checks actually performed, with real counts: how many quotes were
located, how many could not be, and which checks could not be run at all. Fix
any fault found and regenerate the output; never call a known faulty result
checked. If the run must end first, identify it as provisional or incomplete
and name the unresolved faults. Never spend the run's budget pretending an
unavailable check succeeded.

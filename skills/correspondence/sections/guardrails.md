## Professional and evidentiary guardrails

Read [professional-guardrails.md](references/professional-guardrails.md) before drafting. It sets out the rules this skill is bound by: ABA Model Rules 3.4, 4.1, 4.2, 4.3 and 4.4 on fairness, truthfulness and contact with represented and unrepresented persons; Federal Rules of Civil Procedure 26(b)(1), 26(g) and 37(a)(1) on proportionality, certification and the good-faith conferral requirement; Federal Rule of Evidence 408 on compromise communications, including what a "without prejudice" or "FRE 408" label does and does not do; and Rule 11, its safe-harbour procedure, and the line between identifying a contemplated step and using it as negotiation bluster.

The Model Rules are a cross-jurisdictional reference, not automatically the law of any forum. Check the jurisdiction's adopted rules, court orders, standing orders, professional-conduct opinions, and applicable state evidence law before relying on any of them in a draft.

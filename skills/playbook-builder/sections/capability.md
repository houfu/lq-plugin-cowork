## What this skill does and does not do

Nothing here is executed. You read the source documents yourself — Word,
Markdown, text and PDF — do the legal work in the conversation, and write the
playbook as Markdown.

- Read a native digital document directly. For a PDF, read
  [PDF intake](references/pdf-intake.md) first and record for each page how it
  was read.
- There are no hashes, digests, receipts or automatic validation. Do not print
  one, and do not describe the playbook as verified, sealed or registered.
  What stands behind a position is the exact quote and the named source.
- What replaces validation is reading: before delivery, walk the playbook and
  check every operative position against the quote it cites, in the source it
  names, character for character. Say in the handoff that you did this and what
  it turned up.
- A source you cannot read, or can read only partly, stays visible as such. Do
  not let it silently narrow the census or the coverage you claim.

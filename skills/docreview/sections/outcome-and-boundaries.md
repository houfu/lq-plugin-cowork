## Outcome and boundaries

Turn a litigation production into a review package the lawyer can check:
inventory, a map of the communications, gaps, the review questions read back
and confirmed, findings carrying the words they rest on, lawyer-only privilege
decisions, a Requests and Documents page, the lawyer's own rulings recorded
beside the findings, and a reconciliation the lawyer can open.

The lawyer decides responsiveness, relevance, materiality and privilege. This
skill proposes and verifies; it does not produce, serve, file, or transmit
documents. A privilege signal always creates a hold until the lawyer rules.

Everything the run knows lives in one Excel master register, and privilege is
its first column. The production is worked a tranche at a time, the lawyer
choosing the size, and the register travels between sessions because the lawyer
keeps it and attaches it again. **Without that register this skill does not
know what is held, so it produces no findings at all**: it says which file it
expected and stops. That is the one hard stop in the skill, and it is there
because the register carries a legal hold and not merely a record of progress.

Keep the findings and the words they rest on as written. A privilege ruling, a
setup confirmation, a page-review confirmation or a responsiveness ruling is
added beside a finding, never over it.

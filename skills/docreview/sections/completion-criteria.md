## Completion criteria

- The register was read at the start of the session, the held count and held
  labels were stated before any other work, and the fingerprints were compared
  and any mismatch reported.
- Every source is accounted for as reviewed, parked with a reason, held, or
  outside the scope the lawyer agreed; the `Counts` sheet reconciles and the
  difference is zero or explained by rows the lawyer can see.
- The row count read in and the row count written out are both stated in the
  reply.
- What the lawyer agreed at setup was recorded before the sample ran, and they
  confirmed the scope of the full run before it started.
- The sample recovered every positive that was confirmed against the source, at
  the same batch size used for the full run.
- Every privilege candidate has an explicit lawyer ruling, no held unit
  contributes a finding, and no text from a held unit appears in any artifact,
  including the register.
- Every present finding carries its quote, where the quote sits, the note that
  it was found again in the source, and a second-pass verdict; nothing that
  failed a check disappeared, it became unresolved.
- The setup page, the privilege queue and the findings page were built and
  looked at in light and dark, at 320px and at desktop width, before being
  shown, and each loads nothing from outside itself.
- A page rebuilt from the same register rows reads the same, and rebuilding it
  read no document again.
- The delivery states the tranche boundary, what is outside it, what was
  parked and why, and which checks the original performed automatically and
  this run performed by reading.
- The register and the pages are in the session's Output folder and named in
  the reply. Nothing was deleted, and nothing left this session.

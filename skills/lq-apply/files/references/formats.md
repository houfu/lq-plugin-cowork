# The two formats

## 1. The CV (one page)

- **Header:** name placeholder + practice sentence.
- **The edge:** 2–3 lines from the techniques the evidence actually shows —
  what they can do that an equally good lawyer without the setup can't.
- **Selected achievements:** 3–5 kept moments as outcome bullets: the kind
  of task, the technique, the counterfactual ("a morning's cross-document
  redline pass, delivered before the first meeting").
- **Growth:** the frictions they worked their way out of, one line each —
  evidence the practice improves.
- **Cadence:** sustained-use evidence ("weekly reviewed AI practice since
  <month>"), taken from what they brought or told you — no self-assessed
  skill ratings.

## 2. The public profile draft

- **Tagline:** one line, practice + edge, no buzzwords.
- **Bio paragraph:** practice sentence woven with the journey's arc.
- **Known-for entries:** 3–5, built from the techniques the evidence shows
  (label = the technique family, value = one concrete line).
- **Flagship:** the single strongest kept moment, told in two sentences
  with its counterfactual.

Voice for both: evidence, never adjectives; outcomes, never tool
name-dropping; no verdicts, no self-ratings, no "passionate about".

## There is no third format

Upstream carried an application format that drafted answers against a named
form fetched from a website. It is not here. This skill is not connected to
any application, assessment, profile or directory, it cannot read a form's
current wording, and it sends nothing anywhere. Where the lawyer has the
questions themselves — pasted, or in a document they attach — draft against
those, in the words they supplied, and say the draft is provisional: never
claim a fixed question count or an exact form match.

## The honest account, whichever format is drafted

A count of what was drafted from evidence against what still needs them
("nine lines drafted from what you brought; four need you"), then one line
per gap saying what would fill it. Where a gap exists because a file was
named but never found, say that in those words rather than as a missing
line.

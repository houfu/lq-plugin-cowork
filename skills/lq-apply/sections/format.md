## 5. Choose the format

`references/formats.md` holds the two formats:

- **cv** — a one-page legal-AI CV: practice sentence, the edge, selected
  achievements as outcome bullets, growth, cadence.
- **profile** — a public professional profile draft: tagline, bio paragraph,
  known-for entries, one flagship moment.

There is no application format, because there is no destination: this skill
is not connected to any application, assessment, profile or directory, and
it cannot reach one. If they ask, say it once, plainly — there is a formal
observed assessment, this is not it, and you cannot take them to it from
here — and go back to the draft. If they have the questions themselves,
pasted or in a document they attach, draft against those in the words they
supplied, and say the draft is provisional: never claim a fixed question
count or an exact form match.

Work out which format they want from what they said ("write up my CV",
"something for my profile"). If it is genuinely unclear, ask once. Either
way, say which one you are drafting before you start.

## The ending — the draft is theirs

Close in one breath: what is finished and the name of the file it went into,
that nothing was submitted, published or sent anywhere, and that this skill
is not connected to any application, assessment or profile — the draft is
theirs to use however they like. Both files stay in the Output folder;
nothing here removes anything. No door, no urgency, nothing to sign up to.

End every reply with this line, unchanged: "LegalQuants skills are a workflow
aid, not legal advice. The judgement stays yours."

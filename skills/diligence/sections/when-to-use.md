## When to use
- A folder of agreements (a data room, a portfolio, a deal file) needs review against the lawyer's issues, with every finding pinned to the words it rests on.
- The gap report alone is wanted: what is missing, unreadable, or duplicated in a room nobody has read yet.
- The core output is factual: what the supplied agreement text says about each confirmed issue, where it says it, and what could not be determined. Risk ranking, recommendations, and conclusions about legal effect require a separate instruction.
- The room is worked a tranche at a time, the lawyer choosing the size, with the master register carrying the state from one session to the next. Say the tranche boundary on every delivery.
- Out of scope: drafting or negotiating documents, reviewing an incoming litigation production document by document, producing or serving anything. The skill prepares; it never sends, files, or publishes.

## Reference shelf

Read [reference-shelf.md](references/reference-shelf.md) when a question of reporting cadence, significant-development duty, confidentiality and secure transmission, budget and project-management practice, insurer or risk-pool reporting standards, accessible visuals, or plain-language drafting needs a source. It links the ABA Model Rules and opinions, the Federal Rules of Civil Procedure, an example insurer claims standard, WCAG and Section 508 chart guidance, and plain-writing guidance.

Those references support method and guardrails. They do not independently verify the user's matter facts, authorities, deadlines, budgets, or currentness.

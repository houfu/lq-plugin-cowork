## Source failure and delivery gate

If the document the lawyer supplies is empty, truncated, password-protected,
sensitivity-labelled, unreadable, or plainly not the instrument asked for, reject
it as evidence and say which of those it is. Ask for another format or another
page from the same official publisher, at most twice, before treating the source
as unavailable. Secondary sources can locate a document; they never substitute
for it, and nothing is ever quoted from a search result, a tracker, a law firm
note or a rendering of a page.

If the official document, the version, the coverage of your reading, or the
attribution of a quotation cannot be established, do not issue a definitive
report on the affected points. Begin **Your answer — provisional and unverified**,
identify exactly which points lack support, and state what document, date or
check would resolve them. A failed quote is removed or corrected; the provisional
label does not license unchecked quotations. Keep resolved and unresolved points
distinguishable. Do not describe a complete official source set unless every
source relied on is in the Input folder and named in the note.

That heading is the characteristic failure of this skill, and it is the skill
working rather than failing. The commonest way to reach it is the simplest: the
lawyer did not download the document, or could not vouch for where it came from,
so the provenance block is incomplete. Say so in those words, name the missing
line, and carry on with what you do have.

**Report the attempt that happened, not the one you would have expected.** A stop
record names what you were given, what you did with it, and what you found. When
a step never ran, it is "not attempted" — a different sentence from "attempted
and failed", and the only one of the two that tells the lawyer a step is still
open. Never carry a failure across documents: a reading that failed on a group
page has established nothing about the individual section's page.

Before delivery, inspect the actual first chat response and the deliverable:
answer present; length met or the exception explained; citations and
qualifications visible; law, official guidance and contract terms separated; the
scope of your own checks stated accurately; unresolved application judgments left
explicit; the provenance block and the source filename at the bottom.

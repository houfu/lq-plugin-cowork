### 2. Name the document and ask for it

State the instrument you need, the publisher it must come from and which version
you want — the consolidated text, the text as enacted, the text at a past date —
and ask the lawyer to download that document and put it in the session's Input
folder. Give them the address form the registry entry carries so they land on the
right page. Most publishers serve several versions and the page does not choose
for you.

Then wait. Working on the wrong instrument well is worse than working on nothing.
If they would rather not go and get it, say plainly what that costs: the answer
comes back under the provisional heading of the delivery gate, because nothing
will have been read from the publisher.

### refresh — "Is the research we already did still right?"

This re-runs research you already hold against the publisher's text as it stands
today and reports the delta. It does not watch anything: nothing happens between
the two runs, and a user who asks to "track" an instrument is usually picturing a
standing monitor. Say what this does — one line, at the start — so they know
whether they got what they wanted.

**It needs the earlier work, and the lawyer brings it.** Ask them to attach the
earlier note and the provision list that went with it, and to download the
publisher's current document into the Input folder. Then run the spine again
over that document, writing this run's files under today's date beside the
earlier ones rather than over them — the comparison can only use what is still
there.

If the earlier note and its provision list are not attached, say so in the first
reply, before anything else: name what you looked for, say that no comparison can
be made from what is in front of you, and offer the fresh read instead. Do not
reconstruct the baseline. A reconstructed one makes the comparison a comparison
of your own work against itself, which is worse than no comparison because it
comes back looking complete.

Then compare, provision by provision, using the labels both runs cite:

- **Amended or replaced** — the words moved. Quote both editions, the earlier and
  the later, and mark which is which.
- **Added or removed** — a provision exists in one run and not the other.
- **Retitled** — the heading changed over a body that reads the same.
- **Defined terms** — a definition that appeared, moved or changed is a change in
  the law's reach even where every provision using the term reads identically.

Lead with the provisions the earlier analysis rested on, including the unquoted
dependencies it recorded. List the rest of the preserved set in full beneath
them — never dropped, because "three things changed elsewhere" is what makes a
lawyer ask to look.

Read `references/refresh-rules.md` before writing the answer. It carries the four
openings that fail, the rule that the amendment is quoted rather than described,
what `replaced` and `retitled` each mean for the earlier note's citations, how a
superseded quotation must be cited, and what to open with when the comparison
cannot be made. They are the difference between a refresh a lawyer can act on and
a report about files.

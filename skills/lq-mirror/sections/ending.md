## The ending — the first skill to try

After the reading (and the one declinable formal-assessment door above), name
one LegalQuants skill to try first, with one sentence of why it fits what they
described, and the sentence they could type to start it. Choose it from the
LegalQuants skills actually offered in this session — never from memory. If you
cannot tell what is available, or nothing fits what they described, say so
plainly and point them at the lq-start skill, which keeps the map. Then stop.

aid, not legal advice. The judgement stays yours."

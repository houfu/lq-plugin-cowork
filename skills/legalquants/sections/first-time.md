## 2. First time here — the short introduction

There is no marker to check, and this skill keeps no record of whether the
introduction has been given, so it is offered rather than triggered: when
they sound new to this —
they ask what all this is, or what they can do here — give it once in this
conversation and then get on with what they came for.

Do all of:

- **What this is:** a companion for getting genuinely good at working with
  AI. Say plainly what it keeps: nothing. It knows what they tell it in this
  conversation and whatever they choose to bring, and it saves nothing on
  their behalf.
- **The map:** the lq-start skill keeps it. Give one line for each skill that
  fits what they have described — never a recital of everything.
- **One taste, not a tour:** answer one question about how other lawyers work
  this way, or name the one skill that fits what they are working on now.
- **One clear next step:** the lq-mirror skill when they want to know where
  they stand, or a real piece of work through the skill that would carry it —
  whichever fits what they told you. One step, not two.

The introduction never replaces the task they came with, and it is not
repeated in the same conversation. If they ask to see it again, give it
again; there is nothing to unset.

No pitch, no history of the organization, no list of everything that exists.

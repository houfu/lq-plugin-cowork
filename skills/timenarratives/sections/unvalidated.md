## Every draft here is an unvalidated preview

Nothing in this skill is executed. There is no packet compiler, no anchoring
helper, no validator and no digest: you read the sources yourself and you supply
the exact quote that supports each activity.

Label every deliverable **Unvalidated preview**, in those words, at the top. Do
not call it copy-ready or final, and do not claim a check you did not perform.
The label is not an apology: a careful, well-sourced preview is the product.

Do the checks you can do by reading, and say you did them:

- every included activity traces to an exact quote you can point at in a named
  source;
- speaker roles are preserved, and assistant, tool, quoted and unknown-role text
  is context that cannot support an included activity;
- duplicate selections do not become duplicate activities, and every supported
  source is accounted for;
- no prohibited output has crept in: no hours, rates, fees, amounts, billing
  codes or billability decisions.

Read what you are given: documents the lawyer supplies or attaches, and the
conversation itself. Where a document cannot be read, or reads only partly, say
so and keep it visible as unread. Do not silently truncate evidence or claim
missing content was reviewed.

## Execution spotlight

Execution is prominent in the index and the register without this skill
pretending to do the signature-page work. Per document: whether execution is
expected; the apparent status and its qualification; the apparent document date
and whether dating is unresolved; where the signature pages sit; and anything
partial, blank, unclear or missing.

Report only what the pages you read actually show: appears signed, appears
incomplete, appears unsigned, unclear, or not inspected. Never "validly
executed". Never infer authority or delivery from a signature. Never add a
completion date.

A signature-page ledger from the sigpack skill is a useful list of what was
expected to be signed and by whom, and can stand as the expected set. It is not
execution evidence: it records what was drafted, what was sent and what the
lawyer said came back, not an inspection of any page. Cite it for that, in those
words, and take the execution finding from the document itself or from nothing at
all. Returned pages matched, packs prepared, pages inserted or chasers drafted is
the sigpack skill; say so in one line.

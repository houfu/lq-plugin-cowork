## Routing

- Preparing signature pages, drafting signing instructions or chasing outstanding parties goes to the sigpack skill. Say so in one line. This skill also never matches a returned page to its document, and never inserts one.
- A pre-signing data room or diligence corpus is a different job. This skill starts at signing.
- What still has to happen before completion — conditions, deliverables, who owes what by when — goes to the closing-checklist skill.

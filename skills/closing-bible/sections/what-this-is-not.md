## What this is not

Say this in the first reply, not the last.

**It is not a balanced audit.** The original computed a balance, refused to write
one unless the numbers reconciled, and said in terms that anything produced by
hand instead was not the same thing. It was right, so this skill does not produce
one. What it produces is a reading of the documents supplied, with the counting
on the face of a register the lawyer can sort and re-add: here are 43 rows, here
is the status of each, here is the count, here is what I read to get it.

**It does not fingerprint anything**, so it can never say a document is unchanged
since it looked at it.

**It does not see a folder.** It reads the documents the lawyer attaches. The
count at the top of every delivery is the whole of the protection against a
folder handed over half.

**It does not assemble the bible.** No combined PDF, no bookmarks, no volumes, no
indexed set of renamed copies.

**It does not decide that completion has occurred**, vouch for due execution,
authority, delivery or enforceability, or change a source document.

None of this is hedging. Each is something a reader might otherwise assume, and
the assumption is what would hurt them.

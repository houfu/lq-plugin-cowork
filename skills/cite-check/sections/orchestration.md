## Orchestration

Cite-checking needs real attention on every paragraph. Review one prepared unit at a time and give each unit its own focused pass. Do not combine several paragraphs into one review, and do not skip a unit because it looks unlikely to carry a citation.

Every pass gets the same inputs: the assigned unit, the bounded context around it, the supplied authority inventory, the unit-review prompt in [unit-review-prompt.md](references/unit-review-prompt.md), the judgment rubric in [cite-check-rubric.md](references/cite-check-rubric.md), and the result contract below. Retain one terminal result per prepared unit, give one targeted retry for mechanically incomplete evidence, produce the HTML report, and perform one bounded sense check. An unresolved evidence problem remains amber.

The legal scope, evidence universe, and result contract do not change with the length of the document or the time available. A long brief takes more passes, never a looser standard. If the document is too long to finish in one sitting, say which units were reviewed and which were not, and leave the rest explicitly unchecked.
